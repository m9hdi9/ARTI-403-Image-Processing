# ARTI 403: Image Processing - Lab 2

This repository contains the practical implementation of **Lab 2: Digital Image Fundamentals** for the course **ARTI 403 (Image Processing)** at the College of Computer Science and Information Technology, Department of Computer Engineering.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this laboratory session is to understand how digital images are represented, sampled, quantized, and processed using standard algebraic and set-theoretic operations. 

Key areas covered include:
* **Image Sampling:** Studying the degradation of spatial resolution and pixelation effects.
* **Image Quantization:** Reducing intensity levels and observing false contouring artifacts.
* **Arithmetic Operations:** Applying pixel subtraction and scalar addition using saturation arithmetic.
* **Set Operations:** Implementing set difference, symmetric difference, and intersection on binary masks derived from grayscale images.
